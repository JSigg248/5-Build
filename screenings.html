Sigg in the City

In the bustling, glamorous streets of Manhattan, Sigg in the City follows Sigg—a witty, unapologetically eccentric newcomer with big dreams and an even bigger personality—as they navigate the chaotic world of high-stakes fashion, modern romance, and relentless ambition. Armed with sharp humor and a knack for turning every minor inconvenience into an unforgettable spectacle, Sigg quickly disrupts the lives of New York’s elite while searching for genuine connection in a city that never sleeps. Blending sharp comedy with heartfelt moments, this vibrant urban tale proves that sometimes the best way to conquer the Big Apple is to completely rewrite the rules.

Grid Down

When a sudden, coordinated cyberattack plunges the entire nation into darkness, society begins to unravel in a matter of hours. In Grid Down, a former utility engineer must navigate the lawless, blacked-out streets of a collapsing city to reunite with his estranged daughter before emergency reserves run dry. With communication networks dead, financial systems frozen, and desperate factions fighting for dwindling resources, survival depends on outsmarting both the unseen architects of the blackout and the chaos they unleashed.

Kingdom of the Clouds

High above a world shrouded in endless mist lies the Kingdom of the Clouds, a breathtaking realm of floating islands, wind-carved palaces, and ancient magic. For centuries, the sky-dwellers have lived in peaceful isolation, protected by a sacred barrier powered by the heart of the storms. When a reckless young cartographer from the surface accidentally breaches the veil, he uncovers a dark conspiracy threatening to shatter the sky and send the floating empire crashing to the earth below. Now, hunted by royal sky-knights and aided only by a rebellious cloud-weaver, he must unlock the forgotten secrets of the heavens before the storm consumes both worlds.
